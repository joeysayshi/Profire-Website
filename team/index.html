The team.

Our passionate team of technicians come with a wide range of skill sets and specialities, and love including our clients in the process of keeping every system safe, operational and compliant.

Our story

And so, Profire was born.

The beginning

Two driven family men

Profire was started by two passionate, driven family men who were running an LED lighting company throughout Australia, building relationships with strata companies, commercial property managers and large building management companies.

The question

"Can you certify our emergency and exit lighting?"

That question about their AFSS came up more and more. Driven by a dedication to making clients' lives simpler, the best path forward was clear: an all-encompassing fire compliance service.

The team

The best in their field

The directors scouted passionate, experienced certifiers (Accredited Practitioners, Fire Safety), fire safety systems technicians and office staff who were just as obsessed with customer service as they are.

Today

50+ valued team members

From humble beginnings with a small team to over 50 members today, providing fire compliance services across New South Wales. "We are so proud of the Profire team we have today."

Certifiers & office

Here to work alongside you.

With a deep appreciation that feeling safe at home with your loved ones is the ultimate peace of mind, our Accredited Practitioners in Fire Safety lead our technicians in conducting the most thorough and detailed routine fire safety inspections for AFSS.

Our Head Certifiers ensure comprehensive reporting of all defects, using industry-leading software to produce accurate, detailed defect repair quotes and photographic reports with the utmost transparency.

Our specially trained office staff are readily available to answer all questions about your building's fire compliance, under the guidance of our certifiers.
