Fire emergency? Call 000.
If you need Emergency Services to attend a fire in your property, contact 000 directly. We do not offer emergency services in the case of a fire.

If a fire safety system has failed or been damaged and needs urgent attention, call 02 9948 4494 or email office@profireaustralia.com.au to organise a technician, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Call-outs are billed in addition to any maintenance contract, and after-hours rates apply outside normal business hours.
