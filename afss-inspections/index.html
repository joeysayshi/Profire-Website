The AFSS process

Every property has its own schedule.

If your building or property is required to submit an Annual Fire Safety Statement to Council, you (the property owner) must engage a competent and Accredited Fire Safety Practitioner (AFSP) to oversee the AFSS process. The AFSP assesses the fire safety systems installed in your property and certifies that they reach the minimum standards of performance set out in Council's Fire Safety Schedule and AS1851.

Each property is unique, so each has a unique Fire Safety Schedule (FSS), a list of fire safety equipment. These systems must be installed and operational for Council to be satisfied that the property and its occupants are adequately protected in a fire emergency.

The AFSS process requires a complete fire safety inspection of all fire safety equipment in the property every year. Any defects or failures found during the routine inspection must be reported to the owners, who must resolve them before submitting the AFSS to Council.

We specialise in facilitating the AFSS process for strata plans, commercial properties, hospitals, hotels and privately owned residential and commercial properties across NSW. We test all systems to AS1851, in conjunction with the minimum standards of performance on Fire Safety Schedules.

Time-sensitive

90 days from assessment.

Council allows a 90-day window from the date of assessment to submit a complete AFSS. If it isn't submitted by the due date, Council can issue fines weekly until it is.

Our office has dedicated staff whose focus is making sure all inspections, reporting and repairs are completed with ample time for you to submit and avoid fines.
