About Profire.

At Profire we pride ourselves on providing an incomparable level of personalised, friendly and approachable fire compliance service to all of our clients across New South Wales.

What we do

Profire specialise in.

  1. Cost effective fire safety & maintenance.
  2. Facilitating Annual Fire Safety Statement certification (AFSS) across NSW.
  3. Inspecting & testing all fire safety equipment in strata plans, commercial, retail, hospitals, hotels, factories, government and airport buildings.
  4. Undertaking all fire safety equipment repairs: smoke alarms, fire doors, emergency lighting, exit lighting, passive fire, extinguishers, warning and operational signage, mechanical air handling systems, diesel pumpsets, sprinkler systems, hydrant systems, automatic detection systems & building occupant warning systems, just to name a few.
  5. Providing consistent, professional and accurate advice on fire safety legislation and requirements.

Our highly trained team of technicians, certifiers and dedicated office staff are driven to provide the clarity and reliability needed to ensure the people and properties we maintain are protected and certified to the highest standards in accordance with AS 1851.

We are committed to offering the most competitive pricing for ongoing annual fire maintenance and for fire safety equipment and systems repairs across the industry.

We have technicians available and on call 24/7 to rectify any unexpected fire system faults.

We use premium software made specifically for the fire compliance industry to deliver comprehensive defect repair quotes and reports, with supporting photographic images, following all routine inspections, so every defect is reported transparently.
